⚔️ Welcome to ASTRAEA: CELESTIAL ⚔️

You awaken in a strange clearing with no memory. Seek the Merchant for a weapon, then brave the dungeons.

  • Controls: WASD = Move | [E] = Interact & Attack | [Q] = Bomb | [B] = Boomerang
  • Combat: Cut down hordes of monsters. STAND STILL to raise your shield.
  • Exploration: Cut grass for supplies. Bomb cracked walls to find hidden artifacts!
  • The Mission: Gather 3 Shards to dispel the magical barriers blocking the Northern Altar.
